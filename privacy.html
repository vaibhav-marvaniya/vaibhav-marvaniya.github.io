This portfolio uses optional Google Analytics to understand which public pages and project links visitors find useful.

What is measured

If you choose "Allow analytics," the site loads Google Analytics 4 (Google tag) and measures page views, navigation, project-store link clicks, resume views/downloads and contact-link interactions. Project search tracking records result counts, not the text you typed.

How to manage your choice

Before you choose, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Your preference is remembered in browser local storage. Choose "No thanks" to use the site without analytics, or use "Analytics preferences" in the footer to change your selection. Enabling analytics may use Google cookies and process data under Google's policies.
