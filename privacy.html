PRIVACY / WEBSITE ANALYTICS

Your browsing. Your choice.

The site uses optional analytics only after your consent. It has no contact form, no user accounts and no advertising.

Optional Google Analytics 4

With your permission, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits and interactions with portfolio links, public project listings and resume links, plus the selected visual theme. GA4 may set cookies and process your data in accordance with Google's policies.

Search and preferences

Project searches occur locally in your browser. Search text is not sent to Google Analytics; only the number of results may be measured after consent. You can select “No thanks” to browse without analytics or reset your choice using “Analytics preferences” in the footer.

External links

Links to Google Play, Apple App Store, LinkedIn and third-party archives open external websites governed by their own policies. Email links open your chosen email app.
